# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully and constructively.

Good contributions focus on the problem, the evidence, and the implementation rather than the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and sharing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Maintainer response

The maintainer may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this standard.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through GitHub rather than opening a public issue with sensitive details.
